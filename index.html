01 — Practice

A small studio for companies that intend to be recognised, not noticed.

Strategy sits in the same room as typography. We design the part of a company the world actually meets: the name, the system, the site people use when nobody is presenting.

Most of the work is deciding what not to add.

04 — Approach

Three movements. No theatre.

01

Listen

A short engagement to name the real constraint: who it is for, what the category already shouts, and what this company must never sound like.

02

Shape

Identity, language, and a system small enough to keep. Type, colour, and rules — so later work does not drift the moment we leave the room.

03

Make

The site, the catalogue, the materials around a launch. We stay through the first weeks it is in the world, then leave you something your team can hold.

How to rework this template

Colours live in the :root block of the style tag. Change --bg, --fg, and --accent once — ink and paper both read the same names. Type is set with Cormorant Garamond for titles and Inter for text.

Every section is marked with START / END comments and a data-section attribute. Delete one block and paste another. Keep the id if the navigation points at it.

Photographs sit beside this file under /work. Replace the files, or change the src on each image. Case notes live in the script at the bottom, keyed by data-case.
